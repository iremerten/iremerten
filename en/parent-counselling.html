Areas of work

Parent counselling

Parent counselling consists of sessions in which concerns about a child or adolescent, and difficulties met in the course of parenting, are talked through with the parents.

Sessions explore the meanings behind the child’s behaviour and the relationships within the family. Counselling can take place on its own or alongside the child’s or adolescent’s therapy.

Sessions are held in person or online.

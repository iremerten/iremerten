Areas of work

Adult psychotherapy

Adult psychotherapy consists of regular, ongoing sessions that a person begins of their own accord.

In the psychoanalytic approach, sessions are set up as a space where the client can speak freely about whatever comes to mind. The meanings behind symptoms, recurring patterns in relationships and the person’s bond with their own history emerge within this speech.

Sessions are held in person or online.

When do people seek help?

Like every story, each adult’s reasons for coming to therapy are their own. Sometimes anxiety, unhappiness, a loss of motivation or difficulties that show themselves in the body come to the fore. Sometimes it is patterns that keep repeating in relationships, at work or in the family, and that never seem to change, that wear a person down. Losses, separations, traumatic experiences or turning points in life can also become a weight that is hard to carry alone.

Psychoanalytic work does not require specialising in a particular symptom. It tries to understand, from a more holistic perspective, what lies beneath a symptom and what the symptom is saying to the person. For this reason, every kind of symptom and experience can be addressed in psychoanalytic work.

There does not always need to be a clear problem. Sometimes a person simply wishes to know themselves better, to make sense of what they have lived through and to form a new relationship with their own story.

How does the process work?

The first meetings are for understanding the reason for seeking help and agreeing how we will work together. After that, sessions continue on a regular basis.

Sessions are weekly. Where needed, the frequency of sessions becomes something that is explored and shaped together over the course of the work.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with the difficulty I am experiencing?

Yes. Psychoanalytic work is not divided by symptom or diagnosis; each person who comes is understood within their own story. The aim is to understand the function of the symptom and its place in the person’s psychic life.

What happens in the first session?

In the first session you describe, in your own words, what has brought you to seek help. You do not need to prepare; we begin wherever you would like to begin.

Can sessions be held online?

Yes. Sessions are held in person or online.

Can sessions be in English?

Yes. Sessions can be held in English or Turkish.

About

I began my university education studying industrial engineering at Koç University on a scholarship. I then turned to psychology and in 2017 graduated with high honours from the Psychology Department of İzmir University of Economics, again on a scholarship. In 2018 I began the Integrated PhD Programme in Clinical Psychology at Middle East Technical University (METU). In February 2026 I graduated from the programme with a thesis based on my work with adults diagnosed with autism.

During my doctoral studies, from 2019 to 2026, I worked as a member of the teaching staff in the METU Department of Psychology. In the same period I worked as a therapist and supervisor at the METU AYNA Clinical Psychology Support Unit. Since 2024 I have coordinated the unit's AYNA Listening Point. I also serve as a reviewer for AYNA Dergisi, the journal of the METU Clinical Psychology Unit.

I have been conducting psychotherapy in Turkish and English since 2019. My clinical work follows a psychoanalytic approach; I am a member and analyst of the Freud Lacan Psychoanalysis Association. Alongside my therapeutic work, I teach clinical psychology courses at several universities and provide supervision to therapists.

Alongside clinical work, I am drawn to the theoretical side of psychoanalysis and value putting my thinking into writing and sharing it. To that end, I have published articles and book chapters and presented at national and international congresses.

I offer individual therapy for children, adolescents and adults, and couples therapy. I work in person or online with people living in Ankara, and online with those living elsewhere. This site is here so that people who wish to begin therapy can reach me, and so that I can share my work, observations and thoughts. If you have a question, a thought you would like to share or would like to arrange a session, you can write to me through the contact section.

Areas of work

Play therapy

Play is the most natural language through which a child expresses their inner world.

In play therapy, the child has the opportunity to express their experiences, worries and wishes through toys, drawings and stories. The therapist listens closely to this play and makes sense of it together with the child.

The process is supported by regular meetings with the parents. Sessions are held in person or online.

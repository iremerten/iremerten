Areas of work

Couples therapy

Couples therapy is a process in which recurring conflicts, communication difficulties or periods of crisis in a relationship are talked through with both partners taking part.

Sessions offer a space where both partners can make their voices heard. Expectations of the relationship, hurt feelings and the patterns the couple has built together are explored there.

The frequency of sessions is agreed together in the first meetings. Sessions are held in person or online.

When do couples seek help?

Like every relationship, each couple’s reasons for seeking help are their own. Sometimes it is arguments that always end up in the same place; sometimes it is no longer being able to hear each other, or a growing distance. Infidelity or a loss of trust, having a child, becoming parents, moving or a loss can also strain a relationship. Sometimes couples come on the threshold of a decision to separate, to be able to think it through together.

Couples therapy is not about finding out who is right. It is about opening a space in which each partner can hear their own part, and in which the couple can see together what keeps repeating in the relationship.

How does the process work?

The first meetings are for understanding the couple’s reasons for coming and agreeing together how to work. Sessions usually continue with both partners present. When needed, partners can also be seen separately.

Frequently asked questions

My partner doesn’t want to come. Can I come on my own?

Yes. You can also talk about the difficulties in your relationship in individual sessions. Sometimes this opens the way to coming together later on.

Is couples therapy about saving the relationship?

The aim of couples therapy is not to reach a particular outcome, but for the couple to be able to think together about what they want and what keeps repeating between them. Sometimes this opens space for the relationship to continue; sometimes for a separation to be lived through in a less destructive way.

Can sessions be held online?

Yes. Sessions are held in person or online.

Can sessions be in English?

Yes. Sessions can be held in English or Turkish.
